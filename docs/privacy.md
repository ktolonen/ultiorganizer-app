# Privacy policy (draft)

> Draft for store listing. Review before publishing; the contact line and the data controller need filling in.

Ultiorganizer Field keeps score and time limits for ultimate games. It has no accounts and no analytics.

**What the app stores on your phone**

- The games you open: team names, the players' names and jersey numbers, goals, assists, timeouts and the score. Local game data is deleted 30 days after a game is finalized and synced, and you can delete a game by hand from Recent games.
- The app token of each server you pair and the scorekeeping link of each game, in the system Keychain or Keystore. They are never written to logs and are not sent anywhere except to the server they belong to.
- Your Timekeeper settings and the running clock.

**What the app sends**

Only to the Ultiorganizer server you paired with: the scores and events you record, with the app token and the game's scorekeeping link. Nothing is sent to us or to third parties.

**Camera**: used only to scan QR codes. Images are not stored.

**Notifications**: used only to sound Timekeeper signals while the app is in the background. They are scheduled on the phone.

**Crash reports** (optional, off by default): if you switch them on, reports contain no player names.
