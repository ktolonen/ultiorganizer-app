# Privacy policy (draft)

> Draft for the store listings. The contact line and the data controller still need filling in.

Ultiorganizer Scorekeeper keeps score and time limits for ultimate games. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting.

**What the app stores on your phone**

- The games you open: team names, the players' names and jersey numbers, game rosters, goals, assists, timeouts, spirit stoppages, the score and the game note. Games are deleted 30 days after they are final and synced. You can delete a game by hand from Recent games, and forgetting a server deletes its games.
- Game lists and game data saved in the background so that games open without a connection. They are deleted a week after they were fetched.
- The field links you scanned, so their games reopen without scanning. A link is deleted when you forget it, or once it has been unused for a week and its game day is more than a week past.
- The app token of each server you pair and the scorekeeping link of each game, in the system Keychain or Keystore. They are never written to logs and are only sent to the server they belong to.
- Your settings, including the scorekeeper name you enter, and the Timekeeper settings and clocks.

**What the app sends**

- To the Ultiorganizer server you paired with: the entries you record and the scorekeeper name, with the app token and the game's scorekeeping link. That server's operator decides how it handles the data.
- To Expo (expo.dev), to check for app updates when the app starts: the platform, the app version, the update channel and a random installation ID. Expo sees your IP address as part of the request. No game data or names are sent.

Nothing else is sent to us or to third parties.

**Camera**: used only to scan QR codes. Images are not stored.

**Notifications**: used only to sound Timekeeper signals while the app is in the background. They are scheduled on the phone; nothing is sent through a push service.
